# Control Flow and Functions

**M2 . Weeks 3-5 . 12 contact hours**

Conditionals, loops, functions, scope, exceptions, and debugging.

---

## Prerequisites

- Completion of every preceding module in this course.
- A working local Python installation, editor, and Git repository.
- All platform exercises from preceding weeks solved and committed.

## Learning Objectives

On completion of this module a learner is able to:

- if, elif, and else, and the role of indentation.
- Decisions inside decisions, and when to flatten them.
- Combining tests with and, or, and not.
- How non-boolean values behave in a condition.
- The match-case statement and its appropriate uses.
- Condition-controlled repetition and the causes of infinite loops.
- Count-controlled repetition over sequences and ranges.
- Loops within loops, and reasoning about their combined work.
- break, continue, and the else clause on a loop.
- The three loop patterns that solve most problems.
- Defining reusable blocks and calling them.
- Passing data in and returning results out.
- Flexible call signatures and their pitfalls.
- Local and global names, and the LEGB resolution order.
- Documenting intent, parameters, and return values.
- try, except, else, and finally.
- Reading a traceback and locating a fault methodically.
